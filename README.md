# Dawra AI Artical

An educational project dedicated to a **free training course on Artificial Intelligence for academic and scientific article writing**.

> مشروع تعليمي مخصص لدورة تكوينية مجانية حول توظيف الذكاء الاصطناعي في إعداد وكتابة المقالات الأكاديمية والعلمية.

## About the Project

**Dawra AI Artical** is designed to provide learners, students, researchers, and academics with practical guidance on how to use Artificial Intelligence tools responsibly and effectively throughout the scientific writing workflow.

The project will gradually include course materials, practical examples, exercises, guides, templates, and web resources.

## Main Objectives

- Introduce the fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence in academic work.
- Explain how AI can support scientific and academic writing.
- Improve research, drafting, editing, and revision workflows.
- Promote ethical and responsible use of generative AI.
- Help participants understand the limits and risks of AI-generated content.
- Provide practical tools and reusable resources for researchers and students.

## Target Audience

This course is intended for:

- University students
- Master's and PhD students
- Researchers
- University teachers
- Academic staff
- Anyone interested in responsible AI-assisted scientific writing

## Course Topics

The initial course roadmap will cover:

1. Introduction to Artificial Intelligence and Generative AI
2. AI tools for academic research
3. Finding and organizing scientific information
4. Using AI to support article planning and structure
5. Academic writing and language improvement
6. Literature review support
7. Citation and reference management
8. Ethical use of AI in scientific publishing
9. Detecting hallucinations, fabricated references, and unreliable outputs
10. Practical workflows for preparing a scientific article

## Responsible Use of AI

Artificial Intelligence should support—not replace—researchers' judgment, critical thinking, and academic responsibility.

Participants should always:

- Verify AI-generated information.
- Check references against reliable scientific databases.
- Respect institutional and journal policies.
- Avoid plagiarism and fabricated citations.
- Clearly disclose AI use when required.
